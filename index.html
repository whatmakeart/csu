Shape Bin

How to Play:

  • Drag shapes from the bin and drop them onto the play area.
  • Stack shapes as high as possible on the ground or platforms.
  • Reach the target height for the level before the timer runs out.
  • Collect bananas (🍌) by touching them with placed shapes for bonus points!
  • Avoid dropping shapes into the water (💧) at the bottom.
  • The bin refills when your stack is stable.
